Where the 1s and 0s came from

  • Early computers were programmed with punch cards — a hole meant ON (1), no hole meant OFF (0).
  • ENIAC (1946) filled a whole room and weighed 30 tons, yet it spoke the same two digits your phone does.
  • A real moth once got stuck in a switch — that’s why we still call glitches “bugs.”

Bits & bytes

A bit, and a byte

8 bits = 1 byte = one letter (this byte is “A”)

A single bit is just one switch — a 0 or a 1. Line up eight of them and you have a byte: exactly enough room to store one letter, number, or symbol.

Data, files & compression

From bits to files

Data

String a bunch of 1s and 0s together and you have data — the raw information a computer reads. A photo, a song, and this sentence are all just data underneath.

Files

When we save data with a name so we can find it again, it becomes a file. Your phone is really just a giant, well-organized box full of files.
